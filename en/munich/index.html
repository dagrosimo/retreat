DAYS 1–2 · MYTH & NAZARENES

Ludwig II’s Dream World & the Nazarene Mystical Plan

Materializing imagination as escape vs. Overbeck, Cornelius, and Schnorr von Carolsfeld fusing Antiquity, Christianity, and Germanic myth.

Seeing Through the Royal Hypnosis of “Athens on the Isar”

Stepping back inside the completed monumental city to watch Spitzweg’s quiet figures inhabit the gaps around palaces and temples.
