2. Hosting & Server Log Files (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

This website is served as a static web application via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you access pages, the hosting provider automatically records technical server log files (IP address, date and time of access, requested URL, browser type, and operating system) to ensure security and operational stability under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Where US servers are involved, transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

3. Locally Hosted Assets: Fonts & Interactive 3D Maps

Local Fonts: All typefaces (Cormorant Garamond, Literata, IBM Plex Mono) are hosted locally on our own web space. Loading any page triggers zero connections to Google servers (fonts.googleapis.com or fonts.gstatic.com) and transfers no IP address to third parties.

Local 3D Vector Maps (OpenStreetMap / ODbL): All vector map tiles, map font glyphs, and MapLibre GL JS scripts for our Germany atlas and city maps are hosted completely locally on our own web space. Viewing or interacting with the maps triggers zero external requests (such as Google Maps or remote tile servers) and transfers no IP addresses to external map providers. Map data is based on OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

4. Non-Binding Pre-Registration (Fahrkarte) & Contact

When you submit the Fahrkarte form, we process the information you provide (name, contact details, preferred city, number of seats, preferred group language, and optional comment) exclusively to process your non-binding inquiry and contact you (Art. 6(1)(b) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Notice regarding Telegram delivery: Upon submission, your request text is transmitted to a Telegram bot to notify the organizer (messenger infrastructure located outside the EEA). If you prefer not to use Telegram transmission, please do not submit the form and instead contact us directly by email or phone (see Section 1).

Your inquiry data is erased as soon as it is no longer needed for handling your request and no statutory retention obligations apply.

5. Cookies, Browser Storage (§ 25 TDDDG) & Analytics

This website sets no cookies and stores no tracking or session data in localStorage or sessionStorage. No cross-device tracking or marketing storage takes place; therefore, no cookie consent banner is required under § 25(2) TDDDG.

No external analytics or advertising trackers are currently active on this website.

6. Your Rights as a Data Subject (Art. 15–21 & Art. 77 GDPR)

You have the right at any time to access your processed personal data (Art. 15 GDPR), request rectification (Art. 16 GDPR), erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR), data portability (Art. 20 GDPR), and object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). You may withdraw given consent at any time with future effect and lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

7. Version of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy reflects the current technical operation of this website (as of 25.11.2026) and will be updated whenever our data processing setup changes.
